# Factotum

Trivia training for a Monday-night pub quiz team.

## Getting help

Questions, bugs, or a clue you think is wrong? Email [cpessagno12@gmail.com](mailto:cpessagno12@gmail.com), or catch Christy at trivia night.

## Common questions

**The team board says to sign in to iCloud.** Team scores sync through iCloud. Open the Settings app, sign in to iCloud, and reopen Factotum.

**I joined the wrong team.** Open the scoreboard, tap ⋯, and choose Switch team. Your scores this week move with you.

**My widget is behind.** Widgets update when you open the app and at midnight. Open Factotum to bring them up to date.

**Can I add my own cards?** No. Every deck is written and checked by hand, and new ones arrive with app updates.
