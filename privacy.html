Privacy policy

Factotum · Effective November 6, 2026

Factotum is a trivia-training app made for a group of friends. It has no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, and it doesn't sell or share your information with anyone.

What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your study progress, review history, settings, and results are stored on your device. If you're signed in to iCloud, they're also saved in your own private iCloud account, so they sync between your devices. Only you can see that data. The developer can't.

What other players see

If you join a team, Factotum saves a few things in the app's shared iCloud database so your team board works:

  • the name you choose to show on the board,
  • which team you're on,
  • your Daily Challenge and Team Buzzer scores, and
  • an anonymous iCloud ID that tells players apart. It doesn't include your Apple ID email or phone number.

Other Factotum players can see your chosen name, team, and scores. Nothing else is shared.

Removing your information

You can change your name on the board at any time from the scoreboard's ⋯ menu. To have your team board entries deleted, email cpessagno12@gmail.com.

Children

Factotum isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
